EON 的版本更新记录。 Release history for EON.

v1.1.1

最新Latest 2026-07-20

New

  • Import Subscriptions: bring in your data from a generic CSV or an EON-exported JSON file — EON matches the columns for you, then lets you preview, fix, and skip likely duplicates before importing.
  • iCloud icon sync now shows progress, so you can tell how many icons are still on the way instead of guessing.

Improved

  • The This Month widget now lists charges that haven’t been billed yet first, and shows how much is still due this month.
  • Refined the monthly / quarterly / yearly view of Spending Trends.

Fixed

  • Widget icons stayed as letters even after the real icons had synced from iCloud. They now refresh once the icons arrive.
  • Long subscription names were cut off in the Next Charge widget.
  • Spending Trends amounts now match your monthly total. History follows each subscription’s real start date, and future months are no longer projected.

新增

  • 导入订阅:支持通用 CSV 与 EON 导出的 JSON —— 自动识别列,导入前可在预览页确认、改错、跳过疑似重复。
  • iCloud 图标同步新增进度显示,还剩几张没同步一目了然,不用再靠猜。

优化

  • 「本月清单」小组件改为优先显示尚未扣费的订阅,并显示本月还剩多少待扣。
  • 优化支出趋势的月 / 季 / 年展示。

修复

  • 修复图标已从 iCloud 同步完成、小组件却仍显示字母的问题,图标到货后会自动刷新。
  • 修复「下次扣费」小组件中较长的订阅名被截断。
  • 修复支出趋势的金额口径,现与「本月总额」一致;历史按每笔订阅的真实开始日计算,不再推算未来月份。

v1.1.0

2026-07-14

New

  • Spending Trends: view your spending by month, quarter, or year on Overview, with period-over-period change and a bar / line toggle.
  • Fetch icons by URL, with built-in Mac App Store search — web services and Mac apps can have icons too.

Improved

  • Unified the selection menus across the app — icon on the left, text aligned.

Fixed

  • Fixed payment methods showing mixed Chinese text and a jumping width in the English UI.

新增

  • 支出趋势:总览按月 / 季 / 年查看支出走势与环比,可切换条形图 / 折线图。
  • 按网址取图标,并内置 Mac App Store 搜索 —— 网页服务、Mac 软件也能配图标。

优化

  • 统一各处「选择框」菜单样式(图标左对齐、文本对齐)。

修复

  • 修复英文界面下支付方式混入中文、显示宽度忽长忽短。

v1.0.2

2026-07-11

New

  • New welcome guide — understand EON at a glance on first launch.
  • Native iPad sidebar that collapses into a top tab bar for comfortable use on larger screens.
  • App Store icon search: added a search button and a flag-based region switcher.

Improved

  • Subscription amounts now roll when you switch Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly.
  • More reliable renewal reminder notifications.
  • Improved date readability on coloured cards in the editor.

Fixed

  • Fixed iCloud icon sync so icons no longer go missing; added a “Repair Icons” button under iCloud & Data.
  • Fixed the Rate App button that couldn’t be tapped.
  • Fixed letter icons showing only a colour block in the calendar.

新增

  • 全新上手引导,第一次打开一眼看懂。
  • iPad 原生侧边栏,收起化为顶部标签栏,大屏更顺手。
  • App Store 找图标:新增搜索按钮 + 国旗切换地区。

优化

  • 金额在切换 月 / 季 / 年 时数字滚动。
  • 提升续费提醒的通知可靠性。
  • 改善编辑页日期在彩色卡片上的可读性。

修复

  • 修复 iCloud 图标同步问题,「iCloud 与数据」新增「修复图标」按钮。
  • 修复「评价 App」按钮无法点击。
  • 修复日历中字母图标只显示色块。

v1.0

2026-05-25

New

  • First release: subscription management, overview stats, categories, and lifetime spend.
  • Multi-currency support with daily exchange-rate updates.
  • Local notifications with a per-subscription reminder lead time.
  • Set an end date and the subscription auto-archives when it's due.
  • Archive and restore subscriptions without losing history.
  • iCloud sync; export to Markdown / JSON.
  • Home & Lock Screen widgets: next charge, monthly total, this month's list.
  • Subscription Personality — EON analyses your habits and assigns one of 12 personality types (AI Pioneer, Efficiency Hunter, Cloud Nomad …), with a shareable card.
  • App icon customisation: Auto (follows system), Always Light, Always Dark, plus 11 personality-themed alternate icons.
  • Appearance settings: accent colour picker, coloured subscription cards.
  • A few easter eggs: shake to spotlight, daily confetti, subscription balls.

新增

  • 首个版本:订阅管理、总览统计、分类与累计支付。
  • 支持多币种,每日自动更新汇率。
  • 本地通知提醒,可按每个订阅单独设置提前天数。
  • 可设置订阅结束日期,到期自动归档。
  • 订阅归档与恢复,保留完整历史记录。
  • iCloud 同步;支持导出为 Markdown / JSON。
  • 桌面 / 锁屏小组件:下次扣费、本月总额、本月清单。
  • 订阅人格 —— EON 根据你的订阅分析出 12 种人格之一(AI 先驱、效率猎人、云端游民……),并生成可分享的人格卡片。
  • App 图标自定义:自动跟随系统亮/暗、永久浅色、永久深色,以及 11 款人格专属图标。
  • 外观设置:主题色选择、彩色订阅卡片。
  • 一点小玩具:摇一摇聚焦、每日彩带、订阅小球。
