EON 的版本更新记录。 Release history for EON.

v1.1.7

最新Latest 2026-08-11

New

  • Trash — deleted subscriptions are kept for 30 days and can be restored anytime; archiving still keeps their spending history, the Trash counts toward nothing.
  • Overview layout — drag the panels into the order you want and hide the ones you never use. The layout syncs to your other devices.
  • Edit, Duplicate and Pin on each subscription card; filter the list by type and status.
  • Categories, payment methods, base currency and favourite currencies now sync via iCloud too.

Fixed

  • Syncing on one device could wipe a just-added subscription from both ends. Sync now merges record by record, so changes from every device survive.
  • Icons fetched from the App Store fell back to letters, sometimes flickering between the two.
  • Pasting inserted a stray space in front; the first letter of Chinese input broke away from the rest.
  • The caret wouldn’t move in the Notes field and the system paste menu didn’t work there.
  • A pause dated in the future took effect immediately, and subscriptions starting in the future were already counted in the monthly average.
  • Occasional crashes when picking an icon or a custom category while adding a subscription.
  • The category picker was blank on Mac.

新增

  • 回收站 —— 删掉的订阅保留 30 天，随时可以还原；归档仍旧保留历史花费，回收站里的则完全不计入统计。
  • 总览布局 —— 面板可以自己拖动排序、用不上的隐藏掉，设置会同步到你的其他设备。
  • 订阅卡片新增「编辑」「复制」「置顶」；列表支持按类型和状态筛选。
  • 分类、支付方式、基础货币、常用币种现在也跟着 iCloud 同步。

修复

  • 在一台设备上同步，可能把另一台刚添加的订阅从两端一起抹掉。同步已改为逐条合并，各设备的改动都留得住。
  • App Store 取来的图标会变回字母，甚至和字母来回闪。
  • 粘贴时前面莫名多一个空格；中文输入的第一个字母掉出来，跟后面连不上。
  • 备注框里光标移不动，也用不了系统的粘贴菜单。
  • 排在未来的暂停会立刻生效；开始时间填在未来的订阅当月就被算进了月均。
  • 新增订阅时挑图标、选自定义分类偶发闪退。
  • Mac 上分类选择器是空白的。

v1.1.6

2026-08-05

New

  • One-time purchases — log buy-once software and lifetime licences as one-time: always active, no renewal date, and never counted in your monthly average.
  • Notes — keep the account, the deal or who you share it with on the subscription. Shown in the list and included in search.
  • Subscription Calendar widget — a full month grid with each charge day marked by that subscription’s icon.
  • Chinese subscriptions can be searched by pinyin, in full or just the initials.

Fixed

  • Search returned nothing when typing with a third-party keyboard.
  • Widgets showed sample data before syncing, and lost their charge markers after the month rolled over.
  • Some subscriptions never delivered a renewal reminder.
  • Spending charts rendered blank when every subscription was priced at 0.

新增

  • 一次性买断 —— 买断软件、终身授权可以按「一次性」记录，永久有效，不算进月均。
  • 订阅备注 —— 账号、优惠、共享成员都能记，列表里看得到，也搜得到。
  • 订阅日历小组件 —— 整月网格，扣费日贴上对应订阅的图标。
  • 中文订阅可以直接打拼音搜索，全拼或首字母都行。

修复

  • 用第三方输入法打字时，点搜索搜不到内容。
  • 小组件在同步前显示示例数据、跨月丢失扣费标记。
  • 部分订阅收不到续费提醒。
  • 所有订阅金额为 0 时，支出图表显示成空白。

v1.1.5

2026-07-29

New

  • Subscription timeline — record price changes, pauses, skipped cycles, extra purchases and cancellation on each subscription; the stats follow along.
  • Redesigned detail page — current status, spending broken down by price period, and every charge in one place.
  • Manual renewals can be marked as paid, with a reminder if a payment goes overdue.

Fixed

  • Renewal reminders were never scheduled for subscriptions created a while ago.
  • The widget’s countdown stayed frozen until you opened the app.
  • Searching with a Chinese keyboard, and Reminders export failing silently.

Improved

  • The subscription list stays smooth with a large number of subscriptions.

新增

  • 订阅事件时间轴 —— 调价、暂停、跳过几期、额外内购、退订都能记在订阅上，统计跟着变。
  • 详情页重做 —— 当前状态、按价格分段的花费、逐笔账单集中在一页。
  • 手动续费可以逐笔标记「已付」，逾期还会提醒。

修复

  • 订过一段时间的订阅，续费提醒一直没排上。
  • 小组件的倒计时不打开 App 就不动。
  • 中文输入法搜索、提醒事项导出静默失败。

优化

  • 订阅数量多时列表更流畅。

v1.1.4

2026-07-25

New

  • Half-yearly billing cycle. Custom cycles now take a number plus a unit — day, week, month or year, so “every 2 months” follows the real calendar.

Fixed

  • Refreshed the offline fallback exchange rates — conversions were off when the app couldn’t reach live rates.
  • Changing the start date after creating a subscription didn’t stick.
  • The overview layout could break after New → Cancel → Spending by category → Back.

Improved

  • Cleaner immersive header on iOS 18; on iPhone the “Add” action became a floating button.

新增

  • 新增「半年」扣费周期。自定义周期改为「数字 + 单位」，天 / 周 / 月 / 年都行，「每 2 个月」按自然日历走。

修复

  • 刷新离线兜底汇率 —— 拉不到实时汇率时的换算结果偏差。
  • 新建订阅后再改开始日期不生效。
  • 「新增 → 取消 → 支出分类 → 返回」之后总览排版错乱。

优化

  • iOS 18 上的沉浸式页头更干净；iPhone 的「新增」改为右下角悬浮按钮。

v1.0

2026-05-25

New

  • First release: subscription management, overview stats, categories, and lifetime spend.
  • Multi-currency support with daily exchange-rate updates.
  • Local notifications with a per-subscription reminder lead time.
  • Set an end date and the subscription auto-archives when it's due.
  • Archive and restore subscriptions without losing history.
  • iCloud sync; export to Markdown / JSON.
  • Home & Lock Screen widgets: next charge, monthly total, this month's list.
  • Subscription Personality — EON analyses your habits and assigns one of 12 personality types (AI Pioneer, Efficiency Hunter, Cloud Nomad …), with a shareable card.
  • App icon customisation: Auto (follows system), Always Light, Always Dark, plus 11 personality-themed alternate icons.
  • Appearance settings: accent colour picker, coloured subscription cards.
  • A few easter eggs: shake to spotlight, daily confetti, subscription balls.

新增

  • 首个版本:订阅管理、总览统计、分类与累计支付。
  • 支持多币种,每日自动更新汇率。
  • 本地通知提醒,可按每个订阅单独设置提前天数。
  • 可设置订阅结束日期,到期自动归档。
  • 订阅归档与恢复,保留完整历史记录。
  • iCloud 同步;支持导出为 Markdown / JSON。
  • 桌面 / 锁屏小组件:下次扣费、本月总额、本月清单。
  • 订阅人格 —— EON 根据你的订阅分析出 12 种人格之一(AI 先驱、效率猎人、云端游民……),并生成可分享的人格卡片。
  • App 图标自定义:自动跟随系统亮/暗、永久浅色、永久深色,以及 11 款人格专属图标。
  • 外观设置:主题色选择、彩色订阅卡片。
  • 一点小玩具:摇一摇聚焦、每日彩带、订阅小球。
