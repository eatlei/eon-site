Release history版本记录

更新日志Changelog

EON 的版本更新记录。 Release history for EON.

v1.1.11

最新Latest 2026-09-18

New

  • A redesigned Add Subscription screen — name and amount sit right at the top in large type. Currency and billing cycle are tappable pills, and one-time purchases live in the cycle menu.
  • Sub Persona has moved to Settings → Little Toys and opens as a sheet, so its buttons are never cut off on smaller iPhones.
  • Archiving a subscription tells you where to restore it.

Improved

  • Ready for iOS 27: the Add button is its own capsule again, and Cancel / Save on the Add screen are always visible.
  • Card colours now match each subscription’s icon.
  • More reliable iCloud sync when switching accounts, on first sync, and when iCloud storage is full.

Fixed

  • Some subscriptions disappeared when re-importing a large list after erasing data.
  • Tapping Filter or Sort on the Subscriptions tab brought up the keyboard.
  • On Mac, the list now fits the actual window width when you make the window narrow.

新增

  • 新增订阅页重做 —— 名称和金额放到最上面，大字直接输入；币种和周期变成胶囊，点一下就能切，买断也并进了周期菜单。
  • 订阅人格搬进「设置 → 小玩具」，改为底部弹窗，小屏手机上按钮不再被挡住。
  • 归档后会告诉你去哪里恢复。

优化

  • 适配 iOS 27：「新增」按钮重新独立成胶囊，新增页的「取消 / 保存」不再看不见。
  • 订阅卡片的底色和图标颜色对上了。
  • iCloud 同步更稳：切换账号、首次同步、存储配额已满都能正确处理。

修复

  • 清空后重新导入大量订阅时，部分订阅会被吞掉。
  • 订阅页点筛选 / 排序会弹出键盘。
  • Mac 上窗口拖窄后，列表会按实际宽度排列。

v1.1.10

2026-09-11

New

  • Built-in icon library — 210 common subscription icons, organised by category, including services you won’t find on the App Store such as iCloud+ and Apple One.
  • Manual renewals can now be paid early. After you mark a charge as paid, Upcoming moves on to the next cycle, with clear feedback when you tap.
  • Erase All Data at the bottom of iCloud & Data, behind two confirmations.

Fixed

  • Two devices each holding an older copy of your data could keep overwriting each other and never agree. Records without a timestamp now follow iCloud, and you can choose to keep this device’s data instead.
  • Due to Pay listed every unpaid cycle from the past year; it now only asks about the latest one.
  • Tapping “Mark as paid” several times in a row could pay several cycles at once.
  • Lifetime spending details showed an average (total ÷ charges) instead of the real charges, so subscriptions with price changes didn’t add up.
  • Icon sync on Mac could get stuck at 1/17.
  • Adding a subscription crashed on the iOS 27 beta.
  • On Mac, collapsing or expanding the sidebar made content jump left before settling, and the New button sat too close to the window corner.

Improved

  • The subscription list no longer plays an entrance animation on every card.

新增

  • 内置图标库 —— 按分类收录 210 个常用订阅图标，iCloud+、Apple One 这类 App Store 搜不到的服务也有。
  • 手动续费可以提前支付；标记已付后「即将扣费」直接跳到下一期，点下去有明确反馈。
  • 「iCloud 与数据」底部新增「清空所有数据」，两道确认后才会执行。

修复

  • 两台设备各自拿着一份旧数据时，会互相覆盖、永远对不齐。没有时间戳的记录改以 iCloud 为准，也可以选择以本机数据为准。
  • 「待付款」把过去一年没标记的每一期都列出来，现在只提醒最近一期。
  • 连点几下「标记已付」会一次付掉好几期。
  • 累计支付明细显示的是「累计 ÷ 次数」的平均值，调过价的订阅数字对不上，现在按真实账单列出。
  • Mac 上图标同步卡在 1/17 不动。
  • iOS 27 beta 上新增订阅必闪退。
  • Mac 上收起 / 展开侧边栏时内容先跑到左边再回来，「新增」按钮贴着窗口边角。

优化

  • 订阅列表去掉了每张卡片的进场动效，打开更干脆。

v1.1.9

2026-09-03

New

  • EON now runs on iOS 17 and later.
  • Text mode for Overview — turn all the cards into a short, neatly typeset summary.
  • 22 more currencies (48 in total), including BOB, ARS, PLN and ZAR; amounts use each currency’s own decimal places.
  • Tap the total to see how it breaks down; group the list by recurring vs one-time; show amounts in two currencies; a summary bar for search results.
  • Deleting a subscription now asks for confirmation.

Fixed

  • Sync could report success when it had failed, and could overwrite iCloud data it couldn’t read. It now does neither.
  • Card view stopped at 40 subscriptions when scrolling to the bottom.
  • Export skipped paused subscriptions, and importing EON’s own JSON dropped several fields.
  • Lifetime spending ranked subscriptions differently on Overview and on its detail page.
  • A device could give up on iCloud icon sync for good after a single failure.
  • Expired trials still showed as trials; the paused date and pull-to-refresh sync misbehaved.
  • Three settings pages were missing from the iPad sidebar.

新增

  • 支持 iOS 17 及以上系统。
  • 总览「纯文字模式」—— 一键把所有卡片变成一篇排版整齐的小结。
  • 新增 22 个常用币种（共 48 个），含玻利维亚诺、阿根廷比索、兹罗提、兰特等；小数位按各币种自己的规则显示。
  • 点总额看明细；列表可按周期 / 买断分组；支持双币显示；搜索结果带汇总栏。
  • 删除订阅前会先确认。

修复

  • 同步失败时会误报成功，还可能用读不出来的数据覆盖 iCloud。两种情况都不会再发生。
  • 卡片模式下列表滑到底只有 40 条。
  • 导出漏掉已暂停的订阅；导入 EON 自己导出的 JSON 会丢字段。
  • 累计支付在总览和二级页里的排名对不上。
  • 图标的 iCloud 同步失败一次后就彻底放弃。
  • 过期的试用仍显示为试用；暂停日期、下拉同步表现异常。
  • iPad 侧边栏漏掉了三个设置页。

v1.1.8

2026-08-21

New

  • Categories are now one unified list — built-in and your own are equal: rename, recolour, change the icon, reorder or delete any of them, up to 24.
  • Precise category search, pinyin search for categories and notes, and search by amount, payment method or archived subscriptions.
  • Your subscription persona now reflects which services you subscribe to, not just their categories.

Fixed

  • Five persona illustrations showed a white box behind the character.
  • Renaming a category in the editor could quietly change its colour.

Improved

  • Opening the subscription list is much faster (it could take over 3 seconds), and switching tabs is smoother.

新增

  • 分类合并成一张表 —— 预置和自建完全平等：都能改名、改色、换图标、拖动排序、删除，最多 24 个。
  • 分类精确搜索；拼音搜索扩展到分类和备注；金额、支付方式、已归档订阅也能搜。
  • 订阅人格改看你订阅了哪些服务，不再只看分类。

修复

  • 有 5 张人格插画背后带着一块白色方块。
  • 编辑分类时只改名字，颜色却被悄悄改掉。

优化

  • 点开订阅列表更快了（以前可能要卡 3 秒多），切换标签页更顺。

v1.1.7

2026-08-11

New

  • Trash — deleted subscriptions are kept for 30 days and can be restored anytime; archiving still keeps their spending history, the Trash counts toward nothing.
  • Overview layout — drag the panels into the order you want and hide the ones you never use. The layout syncs to your other devices.
  • Edit, Duplicate and Pin on each subscription card; filter the list by type and status.
  • Categories, payment methods, base currency and favourite currencies now sync via iCloud too.

Fixed

  • Syncing on one device could wipe a just-added subscription from both ends. Sync now merges record by record, so changes from every device survive.
  • Icons fetched from the App Store fell back to letters, sometimes flickering between the two.
  • Pasting inserted a stray space in front; the first letter of Chinese input broke away from the rest.
  • The caret wouldn’t move in the Notes field and the system paste menu didn’t work there.
  • A pause dated in the future took effect immediately, and subscriptions starting in the future were already counted in the monthly average.
  • Occasional crashes when picking an icon or a custom category while adding a subscription.
  • The category picker was blank on Mac.

新增

  • 回收站 —— 删掉的订阅保留 30 天，随时可以还原；归档仍旧保留历史花费，回收站里的则完全不计入统计。
  • 总览布局 —— 面板可以自己拖动排序、用不上的隐藏掉，设置会同步到你的其他设备。
  • 订阅卡片新增「编辑」「复制」「置顶」；列表支持按类型和状态筛选。
  • 分类、支付方式、基础货币、常用币种现在也跟着 iCloud 同步。

修复

  • 在一台设备上同步，可能把另一台刚添加的订阅从两端一起抹掉。同步已改为逐条合并，各设备的改动都留得住。
  • App Store 取来的图标会变回字母，甚至和字母来回闪。
  • 粘贴时前面莫名多一个空格；中文输入的第一个字母掉出来，跟后面连不上。
  • 备注框里光标移不动，也用不了系统的粘贴菜单。
  • 排在未来的暂停会立刻生效；开始时间填在未来的订阅当月就被算进了月均。
  • 新增订阅时挑图标、选自定义分类偶发闪退。
  • Mac 上分类选择器是空白的。

v1.1.6

2026-08-05

New

  • One-time purchases — log buy-once software and lifetime licences as one-time: always active, no renewal date, and never counted in your monthly average.
  • Notes — keep the account, the deal or who you share it with on the subscription. Shown in the list and included in search.
  • Subscription Calendar widget — a full month grid with each charge day marked by that subscription’s icon.
  • Chinese subscriptions can be searched by pinyin, in full or just the initials.

Fixed

  • Search returned nothing when typing with a third-party keyboard.
  • Widgets showed sample data before syncing, and lost their charge markers after the month rolled over.
  • Some subscriptions never delivered a renewal reminder.
  • Spending charts rendered blank when every subscription was priced at 0.

新增

  • 一次性买断 —— 买断软件、终身授权可以按「一次性」记录，永久有效，不算进月均。
  • 订阅备注 —— 账号、优惠、共享成员都能记，列表里看得到，也搜得到。
  • 订阅日历小组件 —— 整月网格，扣费日贴上对应订阅的图标。
  • 中文订阅可以直接打拼音搜索，全拼或首字母都行。

修复

  • 用第三方输入法打字时，点搜索搜不到内容。
  • 小组件在同步前显示示例数据、跨月丢失扣费标记。
  • 部分订阅收不到续费提醒。
  • 所有订阅金额为 0 时，支出图表显示成空白。

v1.1.5

2026-07-29

New

  • Subscription timeline — record price changes, pauses, skipped cycles, extra purchases and cancellation on each subscription; the stats follow along.
  • Redesigned detail page — current status, spending broken down by price period, and every charge in one place.
  • Manual renewals can be marked as paid, with a reminder if a payment goes overdue.

Fixed

  • Renewal reminders were never scheduled for subscriptions created a while ago.
  • The widget’s countdown stayed frozen until you opened the app.
  • Searching with a Chinese keyboard, and Reminders export failing silently.

Improved

  • The subscription list stays smooth with a large number of subscriptions.

新增

  • 订阅事件时间轴 —— 调价、暂停、跳过几期、额外内购、退订都能记在订阅上，统计跟着变。
  • 详情页重做 —— 当前状态、按价格分段的花费、逐笔账单集中在一页。
  • 手动续费可以逐笔标记「已付」，逾期还会提醒。

修复

  • 订过一段时间的订阅，续费提醒一直没排上。
  • 小组件的倒计时不打开 App 就不动。
  • 中文输入法搜索、提醒事项导出静默失败。

优化

  • 订阅数量多时列表更流畅。

v1.1.4

2026-07-25

New

  • Half-yearly billing cycle. Custom cycles now take a number plus a unit — day, week, month or year, so “every 2 months” follows the real calendar.

Fixed

  • Refreshed the offline fallback exchange rates — conversions were off when the app couldn’t reach live rates.
  • Changing the start date after creating a subscription didn’t stick.
  • The overview layout could break after New → Cancel → Spending by category → Back.

Improved

  • Cleaner immersive header on iOS 18; on iPhone the “Add” action became a floating button.

新增

  • 新增「半年」扣费周期。自定义周期改为「数字 + 单位」，天 / 周 / 月 / 年都行，「每 2 个月」按自然日历走。

修复

  • 刷新离线兜底汇率 —— 拉不到实时汇率时的换算结果偏差。
  • 新建订阅后再改开始日期不生效。
  • 「新增 → 取消 → 支出分类 → 返回」之后总览排版错乱。

优化

  • iOS 18 上的沉浸式页头更干净；iPhone 的「新增」改为右下角悬浮按钮。

v1.0

2026-05-25

New

  • First release: subscription management, overview stats, categories, and lifetime spend.
  • Multi-currency support with daily exchange-rate updates.
  • Local notifications with a per-subscription reminder lead time.
  • Set an end date and the subscription auto-archives when it's due.
  • Archive and restore subscriptions without losing history.
  • iCloud sync; export to Markdown / JSON.
  • Home & Lock Screen widgets: next charge, monthly total, this month's list.
  • Subscription Personality — EON analyses your habits and assigns one of 12 personality types (AI Pioneer, Efficiency Hunter, Cloud Nomad …), with a shareable card.
  • App icon customisation: Auto (follows system), Always Light, Always Dark, plus 11 personality-themed alternate icons.
  • Appearance settings: accent colour picker, coloured subscription cards.
  • A few easter eggs: shake to spotlight, daily confetti, subscription balls.

新增

  • 首个版本:订阅管理、总览统计、分类与累计支付。
  • 支持多币种,每日自动更新汇率。
  • 本地通知提醒,可按每个订阅单独设置提前天数。
  • 可设置订阅结束日期,到期自动归档。
  • 订阅归档与恢复,保留完整历史记录。
  • iCloud 同步;支持导出为 Markdown / JSON。
  • 桌面 / 锁屏小组件:下次扣费、本月总额、本月清单。
  • 订阅人格 —— EON 根据你的订阅分析出 12 种人格之一(AI 先驱、效率猎人、云端游民……),并生成可分享的人格卡片。
  • App 图标自定义:自动跟随系统亮/暗、永久浅色、永久深色,以及 11 款人格专属图标。
  • 外观设置:主题色选择、彩色订阅卡片。
  • 一点小玩具:摇一摇聚焦、每日彩带、订阅小球。
